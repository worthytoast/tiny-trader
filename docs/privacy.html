Tiny Trader Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Tiny Trader is a single-player stock market game played with pretend money. This page explains what the game does with information about you. The short answer is that it collects nothing.

What the game stores

The game saves your current season, your pretend cash and shares, and your best score. This stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, a name, or an email address.
  • It does not use the internet. The whole game runs on your device.
  • It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.
  • It never involves real money. All companies, news, and prices are made up.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission, vibration, for a small buzz when you trade.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the project page at github.com/worthytoast/tiny-trader.
