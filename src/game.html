Tiny Trader

You have $10,000 of pretend money. Each trading day lasts about 6 minutes.

  1. Watch the price bars move.
  2. Tap BUY to get 10 shares when you think the price will go up.
  3. Tap SELL to sell them. Sell higher than you bought to make money!
  4. Finish the daily 🎯 goal to earn ⭐ stars and rank up.

Want more? Try Pro mode in Settings.
Just a game. The companies, news, and prices are made up.

Pro mode

Pro mode has its own $10,000 and more tools:

  1. Six companies, and news that can move the whole market.
  2. It's the same market as Simple mode: same prices, same clock. Shares you buy here can only be sold here.
  3. Pick how many shares to buy or sell, and own several companies at once.
  4. Stop-loss sells for you if the price falls too far below what you paid.
  5. Your shares stay yours overnight. Prices can jump before the next open.
