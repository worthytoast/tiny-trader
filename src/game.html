Tiny Trader

You have $10,000 of pretend money. Each trading day lasts about 6 minutes.

  1. Watch the price bars move.
  2. Tap BUY when you think it will go up.
  3. Tap SELL to cash out. Sell higher than you bought to make money!

Want more? Try Pro mode in Settings.
Just a game. The companies, news, and prices are made up.

Pro mode

Pro mode has its own $10,000 and more tools:

  1. Six companies, and news that can move the whole market.
  2. Pick how many shares to buy or sell, and own several companies at once.
  3. Stop-loss sells for you if the price falls too far below what you paid.
  4. Your shares stay yours overnight. Prices can jump before the next open.

How to make money
  • Buy when you think the price will go up.
  • Sell when the price is higher than what you paid. That's above the yellow line.
  • Good news often pushes a price up. Bad news often pulls it down.
  • If the price keeps falling, selling early keeps a small loss from getting bigger.
