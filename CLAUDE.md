# Tiny Trader: notes for Claude

Read README.md first for build commands.

- `src/game.html` is the only source. `__LILITA__` is replaced with the base64 Lilita One font at build time. Never hand-edit `docs/index.html` or the Android asset copy.
- `window.__tt` exposes state and actions for the headless test and screenshot script. `window.appBack()` is called by the Android Back button.
- One shared market (Nov 2, 2026, user: same prices for each company in both modes, more like the real thing, and no buying in a crashed market then selling in one doing well): `M = {st, min, phase, news, newsBig, newsS, newsSBig, used, gapNext}` holds every company's price, chart, the clock, and news for both modes. `games.simple` / `games.pro` hold only money and shares (`{mode, day, cash, hold, sel, dayStart, goal, stars, streak, wins}`). Shares can only be sold by the game that bought them (`sellShares(t,q,auto,g)`). Pro stop-losses fire even while you're in Simple; Simple shares are sold at 4:00 even while you're in Pro. Every headline moves the Tiny Market a little, so Simple shows all news (`newsS` mirrors `news`).
- Prices slowly pull back toward each company's normal price (`pull` in `step()`, about 3% of the gap per day). Without it the never-resetting shared market ran away (RoboPup reached $3,625 in a 1,500-day sim). With it, over 1,500 days calm stocks stay about 0.6x to 1.6x normal and wild ones about 0.1x to 5x.
- Save data: localStorage key `tinytrader.v5` holding `{market, games, mode, best:{simple,pro}, seenIntro, seenPro, opts}`. A v4 save (each mode with its own prices) is merged by `joinMarkets()`: the market comes from the mode you were playing, and the other mode's shares in companies whose price differed are cashed out at that mode's old price. v3 saves go through the same path. `__tt.s` is a proxy so tests can still read `T.s.st`, `T.s.min`, and `T.s.phase`; `__tt.fresh()` resets everything.
- Trading School: `LESSONS` (basics, trend, levels, exits, news, candles, size), saved as top-level `school = {n: finished, seen: last card shown}`; a lesson's tool shows on the chart once its card is seen (`known(id)`). While school isn't finished, Simple's goal is `{k:"lesson", i}` and passes via `techHit()` (trend = win bought above the 30-min average; levels = win bought near the hour's low; exits = take-profit sale; news = win bought within 5 min of good news; candles = 2 wins; size = end the day up). Passing shows the next card (`lessonPassed` -> `maybeSchool`). After school, Simple goals also include trend/levels/exits. `ind(t)` gives the 30-minute moving average and the previous hour's floor/ceiling; buys record `above/nearSup/nearRes/afterGood` for goals and loss reasons. After the exits lesson every Simple buy gets `stop:2` and `tp:3` (percent); `checkStops()` handles stop and take-profit for both games. `coach()` writes the line under the chart. The engine has no built-in edge for these tools beyond the existing news momentum; lessons say nothing is certain.
- Holdings are `s.hold = {TICKER: {sh, cost, at, bad, good, stop}}` in both modes.
- Simple mode (Nov 3, 2026, user: "make it one market, with the lines going up and down, this is supposed to be a learning game ... implement real techniques"): one instrument, the Tiny Market (`IDX`, ticker `TINY` in `M.st`), opens each day at 100 x the average of the six companies' price / normal price (`a0`), then moves SWING = 3 times as much as that average during the day (user Nov 3, 2026: "make it go up and down more"); only the day's moves are amplified so the level can't run away, rebuilt each tick after them (`idxNow()`, history from `buildIndex()`). Drawn as a line chart (candles after the candlestick lesson, with a Line/Candles button). No company tabs. At most one holding, and one button (`act()`) that buys exactly 10 shares (`SIMPLE_QTY`, user request Nov 3, 2026; was all-in) or sells them all. Simple's profit goal is $20. Anything held at 4:00 is sold automatically.
- Pro mode (Settings > Game mode): all six stocks plus market-wide news, share amounts 1/10/100/Max, separate BUY and SELL, several holdings at once, a per-holding stop-loss (Off/3/5/10% below average cost, checked every tick), shares kept overnight, and a chart with price labels, volume bars, and an "open" marker. A one-time Pro intro card appears the first time Pro is chosen.
- Live engine: `tick()` runs every 1000 ms (slowed down at the user's request; was 300 ms) and is one market minute; a day is 390 ticks; candles are 5 minutes. Per-minute noise is `vol*.09` with volatility drag removed. News: one headline about every 30 minutes across all six companies (about 2 a day per company), plus market-wide news about 1.4 times a day, in both modes: 75% of the move lands at once, then it drifts on or fades back (30% chance). Every simple strategy, including a news-chaser, has a median day near zero. Rerun `tools/sim.js` after changing any of this.
- Loss lessons: after any sale that loses money (including the 4:00 auto-sale), a short card shows the loss, "You paid $X → You sold $Y", one short reason, and one matching tip (made shorter and easier to read on Nov 3, 2026, at the user's request; keep it that short). The toast and money pop-up are hidden while it's open. A "+ Why so much?" button (`lessonMore`, user request Nov 3, 2026) opens `lessonDetail`: loss per share, shares × per-share loss = total, a 1/10/100-share table with your row highlighted, and a line on how 10 shares makes every $1 move worth $10 (Pro: fewer shares = less risk). It starts closed each time. With the trend/levels lessons learned, "you bought under the purple line" and "you bought right under the ceiling" come after stop and bad news. The reason is picked in order: bad news hit the stock while held, the 4:00 close, a sell within 3 minutes, a wild stock, or plain risk. "Remind me again" keeps the cards on; "Dismiss" turns them off. Settings (header button) has the on/off switch, saved in `opts.explain`, plus Start over (needs two taps; it resets that mode's money but not the market, and opens the next day first if the market is closed). The game pauses while either is open.
- Goals and ranks ("make the game more stimulating"): each day a game gets one goal (`g.goal = {k, n, prog, done, t?}`; kinds: profit = 1% of the day's start rounded to $50, wins = win 2 trades (3 in Pro), stock = win a trade on one company, quick = win a trade held 5 minutes or less, spread = own 3 companies at once, Pro only). `goalUpdate()` runs every tick, after buys, and after sales. Finishing it adds a star (`g.stars`); stars give ranks (`RANKS`: Rookie 0, Trader 3, Hot Shot 8, Shark 15, Big Bull 25, Legend 40). `g.streak` counts profitable sales in a row and resets on a loss. Old saves get a goal on load.
- Stimulation effects: WebAudio sounds made in code (no files) for buy, win, lose, news, news on a stock you own, 15-minutes-left warning, goal, rank up, and the closing bell, switchable in Settings (`opts.sound`). A speed button in the header cycles 1x/2x/3x (`opts.speed`, divides the tick interval; the chart glide scales with it). Money pop-up over the trade button after every sale, confetti on wins, goals, and green days, a red screen-edge flash on losses, a rolling money counter, price flashes green/red each tick, a glowing SELL button while you're in profit, a red BREAKING tag for big news (`M.newsBig` / `M.newsSBig`), and a blinking clock in the last 15 minutes. Confetti and flashes respect reduced motion.
- "How it works" guide (user Nov 3, 2026: a more-information button explaining what everything is and how it works): the `?` header button opens `#help`, built from `HELP` (groups The basics / Chart tools / Pro mode, each topic a tap-to-open `<details>`). Chart-tool topics not learned yet show a "Lesson N" tag. Keep its text in step when features change. The game pauses while it's open.
- Ask AI box at the top of the guide (user Nov 3, 2026: "an ask ai button for specific questions"): in the claude.ai artifact it uses the `sample` capability (publish with `capabilities: {sample: {}}`; runs on the viewer's own Claude account, `modelTier: "quick"`, `cache: false`, last 4 Q/A pairs kept). Each call sends `ASK_RULES` (short simple answers, plain text, no real-money advice, stay on topic) + `guideText()` (the HELP topics and lessons) + `stateText()` (money, price, holdings, tools, coach line, goal) + the question. On the website and the Android app there is no `window.claude`, so the same box searches the guide (`searchGuide`) and says real AI is in the claude.ai version. Never put an API key in the page or app. `ui_test.js` checks both, with a pretend AI for the claude.ai version.
- Opening the game starts it right away (user Nov 3, 2026): a day in progress keeps running, a closed market opens the next day, and a lesson waiting from last time turns its tool on without a card (tap the goal bar to read it). The first-ever launch still shows the intro, and Start trading goes straight into play (the intro covers lesson 1). The game pauses when the app goes to the background and resumes when it comes back.
- Simple's line chart draws each 5-minute bar as 4 points (open, low/high, high/low, close) so it shows every wiggle.
- Keep it super simple. That was the user's main request. No real tickers, no real companies, no real money, no investing advice.
- Android wrapper: plain `Activity` + `WebView` with system bars visible and padded (Android 15+ forces edge to edge). Package `com.worthytoast.tinytrader`, target SDK 36.

## Status (Nov 2, 2026)

- Version 1.0 built and signed. Not uploaded to Play yet; needs a Play Console developer account (an adult's).
- Nov 2, 2026: the user asked for it to feel like day trading with "the bar graph that constantly changes", so the turn-based Next Day version became the live candlestick version. Then they said "make it more simple": six stocks became three, share amounts and the 5-day week were removed, and trading became one all-in BUY/SELL button.
- Then: slowed to 1 tick per second; loss-explanation cards with Dismiss / Remind me again and a Settings switch; then "make in there a more complex mode" added Pro mode alongside Simple; then "make the game more stimulating" added daily goals, stars, ranks, win streaks, sounds, a 1x/2x/3x speed button, and win/loss effects; then both modes were put on one shared market with the same prices.
- This replaced Wall Hop as the game the user wants on the Play Store. Wall Hop still exists in its own repo.
- Nov 3, 2026: loss card made short (paid vs sold, one reason, one tip) with a "+ Why so much?" share-math panel; Simple trades 10 shares; then Simple became one market (the Tiny Market, line chart) with Trading School, chart tools, and a coach.
