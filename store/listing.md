# Google Play store listing for Tiny Trader

Copy these into Play Console under **Grow > Store presence > Main store listing**.

**App name** (30 characters max)
Tiny Trader

**Short description** (80 characters max)
Learn how day trading works: live chart, real trader tricks, pretend money.

**Full description**
Tiny Trader is a simple day-trading game that teaches you how the stock market works, played with pretend money.

Watch the live price line go up and down. Tap BUY when you think the price will go up. Tap SELL to cash out. Sell higher than you bought and you make money!

• One big button: BUY gets 50 shares, SELL sells them
• Trade the Tiny Market: six made-up companies rolled into one price, like a real index fund
• Trading School: 7 short lessons on real techniques (moving averages, support and resistance, take-profit and stop-loss, trading the news, candlesticks, and not betting it all). Each one adds its tool to your chart and gives you a task to practice it
• A coach walks you through each lesson step by step, then a card shows how that tool is used in real markets
• Realistic prices: the market moves about as much as a real index, busiest at the open and close, and every trade pays a small spread like a real broker
• A report card after every day: your trades, win rate, a tip based on how you played, and a true fact about real stock markets
• A How it works guide with a Words to know glossary (stock, share, index fund, bull and bear markets, and more)
• Funny news headlines that make prices jump or crash
• Lose money? A simple tip explains why and how to do better (you can turn tips off in Settings)
• A new goal every day: finish it to earn stars and rank up from Rookie to Legend
• Race three rival traders every day: Trend Tina, Bargain Bob, and Jumpy Jay each trade a different way, and the report card shows whose style won
• Collect 17 badges for good habits like using a stop-loss, being patient, and green days in a row, and keep your daily play streak going
• Win streaks, confetti, sound effects, and BREAKING news
• Each trading day takes about six minutes, or speed it up to 2x or 3x
• Ready for more? Pro mode adds six companies, share amounts, owning several at once, stop-loss, and overnight holding, in the same market with the same prices
• Pause any time. Plays offline. No ads, no accounts, no real money, no data collected.

Tiny Trader is just a game. The companies, news, and prices are all made up, and nothing in it is investing advice.

- **Target audience:** 13 and up is the simplest choice. Picking ages under 13 adds the Families policy review.
